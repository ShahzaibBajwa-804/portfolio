01 — About

Passionate about practical technology.

I'm in the seventh semester of a BS in Software Engineering at the University of Management and Technology in Lahore. My coursework covers data structures and algorithms, object-oriented design, databases and operating systems, and I write Java, C++ and Python for it.

Outside the classroom I build complete applications, mostly in Python with Flask. I like the less glamorous parts of software: designing the schema properly, validating input, handling failure cases and writing tests that catch regressions. A project is not finished for me until it has been run, reviewed and tested.

My interests are full-stack web development and the practical side of AI and machine learning. I'm looking for internship and junior developer opportunities where I can keep building and learn from experienced engineers.
